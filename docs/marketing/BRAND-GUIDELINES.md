# Hatchglen — compact identity guide

Internal production candidate, selected by the coordinator after a name/identity review. The user authorized team decisions without intermediate approval. Legal/name/handle clearance is not implied.

## Name and promise

**Hatchglen**. One word, capital H, lowercase remainder in prose. All caps only in the drawn wordmark. Approximate pronunciation: HATCH-glenn. Ukrainian prose keeps the Latin spelling.

**Raise dragons. Wake the valley.**

A little creature becomes a useful companion, and useful companions make a place come alive. Show that change before discussing infrastructure.

## Mark

A folded leaf-wing shelters an egg-shaped negative space. It combines the woodland-dragon wing with the idea of care, without drawing a full mascot at favicon size. Three distinct concepts were constructed; the wing/egg direction was selected internally for clarity and fit. A root-H was more literal lettering; an eclipse-ring was less distinctive.

Final geometry is vector-only: no raster, live type, filter or gradients in logo masters. Wordmark letterforms are custom paths, not a font dependency. The color master and mono variants have the same silhouette. The small-size cut opens the egg space further.

### Space and crops

Leave at least one quarter of the symbol width clear around a standalone mark. For horizontal lockups use the letter stem width as minimum clear space. Social avatar includes its own safe area and survives a circular crop. X header keeps important copy above the lower-left avatar area. Platform cropping can change; preview on the actual account before applying.

### Do not

Do not stretch, rotate or add strokes/shadows to the mark. Do not insert a token symbol, NEAR wordmark or promised price into it. Do not recolor the logo entirely red during eclipse; the game state can change while the identifier stays stable. Use the provided mono versions when one-color reproduction is required.

## Typography

The logo is outlined custom lettering. Editable creative templates use Arial/Helvetica/sans-serif for predictable rendering. The game may choose a rounded sans with Cyrillic support, but typography choice must be tested with Ukrainian text and small screens. Do not rasterize body copy into world art.

## Voice

Warm, concrete, lightly mischievous. Talk about what a player does and sees: hatch, feed, collect, choose, trade, explore. Small jokes come from dragon behavior, not financial anxiety.

Examples:

- “Small dragons. Useful jobs.”
- “That suspiciously warm rock belongs to the fire dragon.”
- “The red sky opens a door. Your nursery stays safe.”

Avoid “revolutionary”, “first ever”, “the next Axie”, “earn while you sleep”, “guaranteed”, token hype, fake urgency and artificial community statistics.

## Publication evidence

Do not publish a screenshot as gameplay unless it came from the running build. Label a render as “concept” or “art direction”. A NEAR affiliation describes the hackathon/build environment, not an official partnership or Foundation endorsement. Name and contract/network details must come from the verified release, never a mock UI.
