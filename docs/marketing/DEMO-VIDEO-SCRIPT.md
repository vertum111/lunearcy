# Final demo video — capture script

Target: 75–100 seconds, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Show an actual build. Keep a full uncut playthrough as supporting evidence; this shorter edit should still preserve the cause and result of every action. No countdowns or token-price footage before the player sees the dragons.

| Time | Picture / action | Suggested English voiceover or caption |

## NEAR section — choose by evidence

**If a real flow passes:** describe the exact action implemented, not the whole roadmap. Example with fields to complete: “This [action] is verified on NEAR [network]. Here is the transaction, and here is its result in the game. Ordinary care stays local and quick.” Record the actual receipt. Do not use this line with a simulated receipt or local-only store.

**If the integration remains incomplete:** “The current playable chapter uses a local economy. The NEAR exchange is still in development; our repository separates the working prototype from that plan.” Show the actual integration status. This is honest but may limit track competitiveness; it is a product decision to resolve before submission, not something editing can fix.

## Capture conditions

- Use a fresh save and keep a reproducible route. Do not seed fake currency or hide a developer console step from a purported normal-user journey.
- For fast recording, legitimate timers may be shortened in an explicitly labeled demo build; state that in the README and demo description. Do not present sped-up footage as normal timing.
- Finish the input, cost and resulting balance on screen before cutting. Do not cut away immediately after an action tap.
- Use a readable pointer/touch indicator. Avoid excessive zoom punches and transition effects; the creatures and world changes supply the motion.
- If there is no implemented audio, use captions or licensed background audio rather than describing nonexistent sound feedback.
- End on the final name and actual build version. Do not add invented player counts, earnings, a token price, or an endorsement badge.
- Recheck official video/post rules at https://nearhackathon.com/ immediately before publishing. Current requirements include a public X demo, @NEARProtocol, #NEARHackathon, and a public repository.
