# Brand brief — internal choice, 31 October 2026

## Product
A short-session dragon sanctuary game: hatch three distinct dragons, give each useful work, craft, trade and explore when the world enters eclipse. A movable isometric illustrated habitat can expand into fog-covered locations. The name should survive expansion beyond the first nursery.

## Audience and attitude
Players who like caring for expressive creatures, watching a place come alive, and making small consequential resource decisions. Curious, warm, mischievous, quietly magical. Welcome NEAR users and people who have never used a wallet. No investment language.

## Working name recommendation
**Hatchglen** (HATCH-glenn): hatch + a sheltered wooded valley.

Shortlist considered internally:

| Name | Strength | Known issue / decision |
|---|---|---|
| Hatchglen | Easy to say and spell; eggs + habitat; not dependent on one chain or world phase | Exact-name searches did not surface an obvious existing game; this is not legal or availability clearance. Recommended. |
| Glimbriar | Light, growth and dangerous edges; evocative world name | Existing Glimbriar Lens/social and fantasy-name uses; harder to pronounce. Reserve only. |
| Wyrmstead | Dragons + a home; broad enough for growing settlement | Existing FFXIV Free Company and generic fantasy uses; wyrm spelling adds friction. Reserve only. |

Domain and social handles have not been reserved or verified available. No trademark clearance is implied. Keep the existing social accounts; renaming is a separate owner action.

## Category conventions to avoid
Literal generic dragon heads, claw slashes, shields, gold medieval frames, coin/price-chart symbols, futuristic neon, a giant NEAR wordmark embedded into the game logo. A chain affiliation is an infrastructure fact to be shown in an about/footer context, not a substitute for game identity.

## Word map
Hatch → beginning / shell / reveal. Glen → shelter / roots / shared ground. Dragons → wings / curiosity / distinct jobs. Eclipse → changing opportunity / doorway / preparation. Intersection: **a protective leaf-wing sheltering new life**.

## Logo concepts
1. Folded leaf-wing makes a roof around an egg-shaped negative space. Recommended: identifiable creature world without drawing a mascot.
2. Two roots form a soft H; the crossbar becomes a nest with one seed. Most letter-led; less expressive.
3. An interrupted ring makes an eclipse/egg, with a leaf notch. Strong in motion; more generic at small size.
4. A root curl makes a G around a seed.
5. Two unequal leaf wings make an opening doorway.
6. A folded shell makes a little hill and the letter H.
7. A tail curl ends in a leaf pointing toward a sun.
8. Three rounded stones surround one clear egg-shaped gap.

The logo will be constructed from vector paths. No game character artwork is required for identification at tiny sizes. The mascot dragons stay in gameplay art and campaign creatives.

## Constraints and checkpoint
User explicitly authorized resolving creative decisions with subagents without intermediate check-ins; the logo skill's normal concept checkpoint is skipped under that explicit exception. The internal recommendation and any remaining risks are reported to the coordinator. No external publication.
